02 Method n = 150 sampled products · HYBRID retrieval

We scored retrieval against your cluster IDs

No new labels were invented. Every score below asks one question: when we search with a product's own text, do the products that come back carry the same PS_PRODUCT_ID?

STEP 01

Sample a product

Draw a product whose cluster contains at least one other textually different product. Identical rows would make the match trivial.

STEP 02

Query with its own text

Send that product's concatenated attributes — vendor, style, description, hierarchy — to the index and take the top k results.

STEP 03

Check the cluster IDs

Count how many returned products share the query's cluster. Exact copies of the query text are discarded before scoring.

How precision is calculated

Precision@k = matched ÷ min(k, products actually available) — we divide by what could have been found, not by k. Half your clusters contain a single product, so dividing by a fixed k would score a perfect result as a failure. Reported on the multi-VSN population: the 38.4% of clusters spanning more than one vendor style number, where exact-match SQL cannot already solve the problem.

Not embedded — used as filters

Six low-cardinality columns are carried into the index for filtering rather than embedding: source region, import/domestic, banner, department and class number, plus export country in its raw form. As a filter the match is exact and cheap; as embedded text it would be fuzzy and could rank a wrong-region product above a right-region one.

Reading this table

Precision is the share of returned products in the right cluster — the practitioner-facing number. Recall is whether any correct product came back at all. On the deduplicated corpus every sampled product also retrieved itself, which confirms the index is complete; on the full corpus 31 of 150 could not, because duplicate text crowds them out of their own result list.

What this means operationally

Collapsing to one row per distinct product reduced the corpus 4.89× — 1,996,496 rows to 408,027 — and made every slot in the result list carry new information. This was not a modelling change. The retrieval was always working; the result list was being wasted.

06 Constraint 109,006 clusters · median 2 distinct products

Half your clusters contain one product

This is the most important finding in the deck, and it is a property of the labels rather than the retrieval. It caps what any score can mean.

Distinct products per cluster

Median cluster holds 2 distinct products. A singleton cluster has no second product to retrieve, so it cannot be scored at all.

Could a majority ever be correct?
Results requestedClusters with enough products
k = 330.4%

For a majority of k results to share the query's cluster, the cluster must contain enough products to fill the list. At k=10 only 12.8% do — which is why we report at k=3.

The honest reading

A system that returns every product a cluster contains and then fills the remaining slots with near-misses is behaving correctly, but a naive precision@10 would score it 0.2. That is why our denominator is what was findable, and why k=3 is the setting we recommend for a practitioner-facing tool.

Vendor style numbers are high-precision

Of 219,194 distinct vendor style numbers, only 2 span more than one cluster. So the pair (vendor, style) can be trusted as a cheap pre-pass — it resolves 61.6% of multi-row clusters outright, leaving the 38.4% we measured semantic retrieval against.

Four defects handled — and worth fixing at source

None of these blocked the evaluation. All four are worth correcting before this becomes a production pipeline.

3,176rows · 0.159%

Field shift from inch marks

An inch mark in a product name (12" CERAMIC WOK) was double-escaped at export, pushing every later value one column right. Quarantined — their row IDs are unusable.

571rows

Country field holds port names

Export country mixes ISO3 codes with ports of lading: US PORT (456), NINGBO, GUANGZHOU, FELIXSTOWE, UK. Two concepts in one column.

64.4%missing, but structural

Nullity that leaks a flag

Export country is absent on 59.8% of domestic rows and 0% of imports. Left raw it teaches the model import-versus-domestic instead of product identity, so we gate it.

171krows with DNU markers

Retired hierarchy values

"DNU" placeholders remain in category (117,644) and class (53,349) names. Treated as low-trust context rather than real categories.
